Terms of Service
Last updated: June 2025
1. Eligibility
You must be a currently enrolled university student with a valid institutional (.edu) email address to use UniMeet. By registering, you confirm that you meet this requirement.
2. How Matching Works
When you initiate a match, the app searches for other active users within a 3 mile radius. If a match is found, both users have 5 minutes to chat. Leaving the chat screen ends the session immediately.
3 mile radius
5 min session
Exit = session ends
3. Anonymity & Identity
All users are fully anonymous during chat. No names, photos, or personal details are shared automatically. Identity may only be revealed if both parties mutually agree to meet.
4. Credits & Purchases
Match credits are non-refundable once consumed. Purchases are processed via App Store or Google Play. Credits earned via ads have no monetary value.
5. Prohibited Conduct
Harassment, hate speech, impersonation, and any illegal activity are strictly forbidden and will result in immediate and permanent account termination.
No harassment
No impersonation

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2025
1. Data We Collect
We collect your institutional email address (for verification only), approximate location (during active sessions only), and in-app activity such as match frequency. We never store chat messages.
.edu email
Approx. location
No messages stored
2. Location Data
Your precise location is never stored or shared. We only use it in real-time to find nearby users within a 3 mile radius. Location access is active only during an open session.
3. Anonymity
Your identity is never revealed to other users without explicit mutual consent. Your .edu email is used solely for account verification and is never visible to other users.
4. Data Retention
Chat messages are permanently deleted at the end of each session. Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request full deletion at any time.
Chats auto-deleted
Delete anytime
5. Third Parties
We use App Store / Google Play for payments. We do not sell your data to any third party. Ad networks receive only anonymous, non-identifiable signals.
