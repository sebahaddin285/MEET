UniMeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 · Developer: Stear · Contact: unimeet64@gmail.com

The short version

  • UniMeet connects university students who are physically nearby for a short, anonymous chat.
  • We use your institutional email only to verify you. Other users never see it.
  • We use your device location only while you are searching for a match, and remove it when the search ends.
  • Chat messages are deleted when the chat ends.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see Delete your account).

1. Who we are

UniMeet (the “app”) is developed and operated by Stear (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. By using UniMeet you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhat it is and why we need it
Institutional email address You enter it to sign in. We email you a one-time verification code and use the address to identify your account. It is stored in our authentication system and in your account record. It is never shown to other users.
Verification and abuse-prevention records The one-time code is stored only as a salted hash and expires after 10 minutes. To limit abuse we also keep counters that are keyed by a hash of your email address and of the network address (IP) a request came from, so the raw values are not stored in these records.
Account information A randomly generated anonymous name, your match-credit balance, whether you are currently in a chat, and when the account was created.
Device location When you start a search, the app reads your device location (typically accurate to roughly 100 meters) and sends it to our servers so we can find other users within 3 miles. We only read your location while the app is open and you have asked to match. We never collect location in the background. The distance between two matched users is saved as a number; the coordinates are not.
Chat content Text messages you send, and a location only if you choose to share one in the chat. This content is visible to the person you are chatting with and is stored on our servers while the chat is active.
Advertising and device information If you watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob collects information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with the ad (see Advertising).
Technical logs Our cloud provider records operational logs (for example timestamps, request metadata, error messages and your internal account identifier) so we can keep the service running and investigate abuse. We do not write your coordinates or message text to these logs.

What we do not collect: your real name, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or your location when the app is closed.

3. How we use information

  • To verify that you are a student and to sign you in.
  • To match you with nearby users and let you chat.
  • To manage your match credits, including credits earned by watching ads.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud and violations of our Terms of Service, and to keep the service secure.
  • To show ads that fund the app.
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your chat messages for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Other users

The person you are matched with sees only what you send in the chat, and you appear to them only as “Anonymous User”. They can copy or screenshot anything you send, and we cannot prevent that, so please be careful about what you share, especially your location.

Service providers

We use Google services to run the app. They process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide those services:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Secret Manager, Cloud Logging) to store data and run the app’s backend.
  • Gmail to send your verification code by email.
  • Google AdMob to show rewarded ads (see below).

Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and, for Firebase, at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if we believe it is required by law or legal process, or necessary to protect the safety of users or the public, or to protect our rights. If the app is ever sold or transferred, information may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy.

5. Advertising

UniMeet lets you earn match credits by watching rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads, under Google’s policies. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before collecting this information.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or in the corresponding iOS settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The app does not currently sell in-app purchases. If we add them, payments will be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we will not receive your payment card details.

6. How long we keep information

InformationHow long
Location used for matchingRemoved as soon as the search ends (a match is made, you cancel, or it times out). If the app is closed unexpectedly, it is removed automatically within about 30 minutes.
Chat messages and any location shared in a chatDeleted when the chat ends. If a chat is abandoned, it is deleted automatically after 24 hours at the latest.
Verification code (hash) and abuse-prevention countersThe code expires after 10 minutes; these records are deleted automatically within about 2 days.
Account information and email addressUntil you ask us to delete your account.
Operational logsUp to 30 days.
Advertising dataHandled by Google under its own retention policies.

We may keep limited information longer if we are legally required to, or need it to resolve a dispute or enforce our terms.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can turn off location permission at any time in your device settings. Matching will not work without it.
  • Ads: you can limit ad personalization and reset your advertising ID as described above.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California and some other U.S. states), you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Delete your account

To delete your UniMeet account and the data associated with it:

  1. Send an email to unimeet64@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my UniMeet account”.
  2. Send it from the institutional email address you use to sign in to UniMeet, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your sign-in record, your account record (anonymous name, credit balance, chat state) and any remaining queue or chat data.

What may remain briefly: hashed abuse-prevention counters (up to about 2 days), operational logs (up to 30 days), and anything we are legally required to keep. Your match credits have no cash value and cannot be recovered after deletion.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to stored data is restricted by server-side rules so that you can only reach your own account and your own chats. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

UniMeet is intended for university students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where data is processed

Our backend is hosted by Google Cloud. Your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where Google operates, which may have different data-protection laws than your country.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and for material changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
